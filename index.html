Berdakh Abibullaev

Associate Professor · Nazarbayev University

I lead research at Nazarbayev University’s Brain–Machine Intelligence Lab. I develop models that decode neural signals for new users with less calibration and apply these methods to assistive interfaces. My work also includes AI tools for studying epilepsy with intracranial EEG.
